How to Survive

  • You are a tin soldier with one leg. Reach the door. That is the whole story.
  • Move with ◀ ▶ (or arrow keys / A D). Jump with ▲ (or Space / W).
  • You will die. A lot. That is how you learn what the level is hiding.
  • The floor may rise, slide, shove, vanish, or lie. So may the springs. It starts gently. It does not stay gentle.
  • Restart is instant. R restarts, Esc pauses, M mutes.
